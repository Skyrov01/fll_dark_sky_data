Method

Show the audit questions & scoring
  • 1. Purpose - does the light serve a clear, necessary purpose?
  • 2. Spill - does light reach beyond where it is needed?
  • 3. Controls - is it on a timer or motion sensor? (dusk-to-dawn photocells are discouraged)
  • 4. Brightness - could it be dimmer and still do its job?
  • 5. Colour - warm light (3000 K or less) is far less attractive to moths than cool white / blue-rich light.
  • Shielding - full cut-off (best) > cut-off > semi cut-off > no cut-off (worst).

Each answer adds penalty points (0 to 4). Total 0-3 = Good, 4-7 = Fair, 8+ = Bad. An unnecessary light is always a candidate to remove. Warm-white or amber LEDs, shielded, on motion sensors are the moth-friendly ideal.

Sources: Butterfly Conservation "Your guide to moths and light pollution" and the IDA home lighting audit.
